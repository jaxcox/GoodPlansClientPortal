## 7. FAQ Section

**Headline:**
Common questions.

**Q: How often do we meet?**
Twice a month, 30 minutes each. Less ceremony than a weekly cadence, more momentum than monthly.

**Q: How long do most clients stay?**
There's no contract. You stay as long as you have goals worth pursuing and want a second pair of eyes. Most clients are a year or longer.

**Q: Is the dashboard included?**
Yes. Built specifically for your business. The portal is the centerpiece of how we work, not an add-on.

**Q: Do you use Profit First?**
Yes. It's one of the strongest cashflow management tools you can implement. I run an assessment, build an implementation plan for your specific business, and give you the tools to make it work. It's part of the coaching, not a standalone product. I'm a former Profit First Professional.

**Q: What's NOT included?**
I'm not your accountant, your marketing agency, your tax pro, or your lawyer. I'll point you to the right experts when you need them. I stay in my lane. The lane is financial coaching and operational visibility.

**Q: I've tried coaches before — were they all motivational speakers?**
Probably. Most business coaches don't know how to read a financial statement. I do. If you want feelings and breakthroughs, I'm not the right coach. If you want facts and action items, I am.
