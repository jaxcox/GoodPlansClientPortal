## The pattern

A small business owner is putting in the hours. Working as hard as they can. Doing right by their customers, paying their team, juggling a hundred decisions a week. Revenue is going up. The bank account is staying flat. They can't figure out why.

Their CPA tells them what happened last year, six months after the fact. Their bookkeeper records transactions. Nobody is looking at the actual operating numbers every couple of weeks and helping them figure out what to focus on next.

So they do what any go-getter would do. They work harder. They put in more hours. They take the next job. They tell themselves growth will fix it.

It almost never does.

The fix isn't more hours. It's better visibility, the right system for managing cash, and a second pair of eyes that knows how to read what the numbers say.

That's the practice I built. The portal does the visibility. Profit First does the cash. I do the asking-the-right-questions part. Twice a month we sit down and work it through.

## What I believe

A few principles that shape everything I do.

### I sell facts, not hopes.
No earth-shattering promises. No "transform your business in 90 days." Real numbers you can use to make real decisions.

### Insights and questions, not answers.
Most coaches sell prescriptions. I show you what I see in your data and ask you the questions that point at the real issue. You build the solutions. That's how change actually sticks.

### I stay in my lane.
I'm not your accountant, your tax pro, your marketing agency, or your lawyer. I'll point you to good ones. The lane I work in is financial coaching and operational visibility. I don't pretend to know what I don't.

### Numbers tell the truth your gut can't.
A good week feels the same as a bad week when you're slammed. The dashboard is the only thing that consistently tells you which one you actually had.

### Bi-weekly beats monthly.
Twice a month is the right rhythm for an operating business. Once a month and you're managing in hindsight. Once a week and the work starts to feel like ceremony. Twice a month is when momentum builds.

## Who I work with

Small business owners with a team, providing a real product or a real service. People who are willing to do the work, open to new ideas, read books, listen to podcasts, and want progress they can actually see.

I work with people who can take input and act on it. If you're looking for someone to make decisions for you, I'm not the right coach.
