## About the engagement

### How often do we meet?
Twice a month, 30 minutes each. Less ceremony than a weekly cadence, more momentum than monthly. We add quarterly planning sessions for the bigger stuff (annual budgets, hiring decisions, capacity planning).

### What does the coaching actually look like week to week?
Every other week we meet for 30 minutes. We open your dashboard, look at what changed, talk through what's working and what isn't, and decide on action items. Between sessions you're using the portal and (optionally) emailing or messaging me with anything urgent.

### How long does it take to see results?
Most clients see a meaningful shift in how they think about their business within the first month. Measurable changes in their numbers usually show up in months 2 to 3. The dashboard itself is useful from week one.

### What's the time commitment from me?
30 minutes every two weeks for the sessions. A few minutes a week entering or reviewing your numbers (or having your bookkeeper do it). Quarterly we do a longer block, usually 90 minutes. Total: about 3 hours a month, give or take.

### Do I need my numbers organized before we start?
No. Bring what you have. Owners who wait until their numbers are "ready" wait forever. Part of what we'll do together is figure out what's worth tracking, and the dashboard makes it easier to organize what was previously scattered.

## Cost and structure

### What does the engagement cost?
We'll talk pricing on the discovery call if it makes sense to work together. Not before. The discovery call itself is free.

### Are there annual contracts?
No. Engagements are month-to-month with 30 days' notice to exit. Most clients stay a year or longer because the work is working, not because they're locked in.

### Can I pause?
Yes. If something comes up (medical, family, sale of the business), we can pause for up to 3 months and resume. Just let me know.

## Fit and scope

### Will this work for my type of business?
This works for small business owners with a team who provide a real product or a real service. Most clients are in trades and trade-adjacent businesses. If you have employees, schedules, and bill for your time or your work, it usually fits. If you're a solopreneur, run a restaurant, or you're in a purely professional services field (law, accounting), there's probably a better-fit coach for you.

### What if I'm earlier-stage?
If you don't have employees yet, the dashboard and bi-weekly rhythm assume operational complexity you might not have. Worth a discovery call to talk it through anyway — sometimes the answer is "not yet, here's what to focus on first."

### How is this different from my CPA or bookkeeper?
Your CPA tells you what happened, usually after the year is over. Your bookkeeper records transactions. We talk about what's happening, every couple of weeks, and what to do about it. Different role, different cadence, different toolset. Most clients keep their CPA and bookkeeper and add me on top.

### How is this different from other business coaches?
Most coaches sell mindset, leadership, or sales improvement. I'm one of relatively few coaches who can read a financial statement and tell you what's actually happening in your business. If you want a mindset coach, I'm not the right fit. If you want someone who looks at your numbers every other week and helps you make better operating decisions, this is built for that.

### Do you use Profit First?
Yes. It's one of the strongest cashflow management tools you can implement. I run an assessment, build an implementation plan for your specific business, and give you the tools to make it work. It's part of the coaching, not a standalone product. I'm a former Profit First Professional.

### What if I want help with marketing or HR or taxes?
I'll point you to good people. I stay in my lane. The lane I work in is financial coaching and operational visibility. I don't pretend to know what I don't.

## The dashboard / portal

### Is the portal included?
Yes. Every client gets a custom dashboard. Setup is part of the first couple of weeks.

### What if I don't want to learn new software?
The dashboard is designed for non-technical owners. Most clients are comfortable in it within their first session. If you can use QuickBooks (even a little), you can use this.

### Who else can access my dashboard?
Just you and me by default. You can add additional users (a business partner, an office manager, your bookkeeper) on request.

### Where is my data stored?
On Supabase, an industry-standard managed database service. The portal is encrypted, behind a login, and your data isn't shared with anyone outside our engagement.

## Getting started

### What happens on the discovery call?
30 minutes. We talk about your business, what you're trying to achieve, and how my program works. If working together makes sense, we'll talk through next steps. If it doesn't, no pressure — most discovery calls don't convert into engagements and that's fine.

### What if I'm not ready to start, but I want to learn more?
Email me through the contact form, or read the blog and KPI glossary on this site. Both are free and designed to be useful whether or not you ever hire me.
