## The structure

Three pieces. The rhythm matters as much as the tools.

### Your custom dashboard
I built this portal myself. Every client's version is custom — your KPIs (including ones we create from scratch when off-the-shelf doesn't fit). We start by building a realistic annual budget if you don't already have one. From there, you see pace against your goals — this week, this month, this quarter, this year. Margin, capacity, leading indicators, all in one place. You see what's working and what's about to need attention.

**[NOTE: Dashboard demo video will live here once recorded.]**

### Bi-weekly working sessions
Twice a month, 30 minutes each. We open your dashboard, walk through what's actually happening, and decide what to focus on next. You leave with specific action items — things you actually want to do, not "shoulds" you'll ignore.

### Budgeting & strategic planning
Annual budgets that account for your seasonality, with quarterly goals that ladder up to where you want to be at year-end. Hiring decisions, pricing changes, capacity planning — all easier when the strategy is built on real numbers.

## The first 90 days

The first quarter is structured so you see traction quickly.

### Weeks 1-2: Setup
We build your annual budget if you don't already have one (most don't). We pick your KPIs together. I set up your dashboard. You start seeing your numbers in a clear view, probably for the first time.

### Weeks 3-6: Baseline
First few sessions of real data. We identify two or three specific things worth focusing on. Not fifteen. Three. The goal is wins you can feel by week 8.

### Weeks 7-12: Momentum
Those fixes start showing up in the numbers. We course-correct, double down on what's working, drop what isn't. By week 12 you've seen the pattern and you trust the system.

---

## Cadence after the first 90 days

Same bi-weekly rhythm. We keep tracking, keep adjusting, keep moving you toward your goals.

Quarterly we step back and reset. Annually we look at the bigger picture and set the year's targets together.

## Who this is for

Small business owners with employees who provide a real product or service. People willing to do the work, open to new ideas, and ready for progress they can actually see.

---

## Who this isn't for

- Solopreneurs (the dashboard and rhythm assume you have a team)
- People looking for mindset coaching, motivational speeches, or "breakthrough" sessions
- People who want me to make their decisions for them — I provide insights and questions; you build the answers
- Businesses where the owner isn't ready to look at the financial reality (this work requires honest numbers on the table, even when they're uncomfortable)
