COACH VIEW Dashboard Weekly Entry Budget & Goals History Settings Back
Page body identical to Client View — same cards, same fields, same edit access. Coach can change any goal or budget number on the client's behalf.
Behavior & Logic
Storage
All budgets persist at portal:budgets:<clientId>. Keyed by year. Multiple years can coexist on the same client.
Year tabs
Year buttons at top of page switch which budget object is displayed. "+ 2027" creates a fresh empty budget for next year and switches to it. Once created, it gets its own tab.
Static parts of this page
Annual Revenue Budget, YTD Actuals, Monthly Distribution, and Monthly Financial Goals are always shown — they're tied to always-on financials (Revenue, COGS, GP, GP%) and the budget structure itself.
Dynamic parts of this page
Goal cards by category (Marketing, Sales, Operations, Team) appear only if KPIs are toggled on in Settings for that category. Each KPI toggle drives whether its goal field exists. Toggle off in Settings → goal field disappears here. Toggle back on → the previously-saved goal value reappears (preserved, not deleted).
Capacity & Utilization Targets card appears only if at least one capacity group exists in Settings.
Custom KPI goal fields appear in their assigned categories only when the custom KPI exists in Settings.
Annual budget math
Coach enters Annual Revenue $ and GP % Target.
GP $ = Revenue × (GP% ÷ 100).
COGS $ = Revenue − GP $.
COGS % = 100 − GP%.
These four numbers display in the summary strip and feed every monthly distribution / goal calculation downstream.
Monthly distribution: even vs seasonal
Even: annual revenue ÷ 12 each month.
Seasonal: 12 percentage fields (one per month). Must total 100% (banner shows red if not). Each month's revenue goal = annual × that month's percentage.
Both modes feed into the Monthly Financial Goals tiles.
YTD actuals reflow remaining months
If YTD Revenue + COGS are entered (and "Most Recently Completed Month" is set), remaining months get adjusted to close the gap toward the annual GP goal.
Math: Remaining GP needed = Annual GP goal − YTD GP. Remaining revenue needed = Remaining GP ÷ GP margin. Distributed across remaining months by even split or by remaining seasonal percentages.
Past months show as greyed/checked tiles. Future months show with the adjusted (often higher) goal.
"Remaining revenue to produce" line is shown only when reflow is active.
Status banners
Shown only when YTD actuals are entered and Annual Revenue is set. Two banners — one for Revenue (vs Jan–MarchToDate budget), one for Gross Profit (vs Jan–MarchToDate GP budget). Green if on/above, red if behind. The GP banner adds a note when behind that the remaining goals have been adjusted.
"Month Coaching Started"
Determines which months show in the Monthly Financial Goals grid (Apr → Dec, for example). Doesn't affect the math — just the visible window. Months before the start date aren't shown, even if YTD actuals cover them.
Per-week preview text
Goal fields with $ or # format show a small "~X/wk" hint below the input — the monthly value ÷ 4.33. Helps coaches sanity-check whether the monthly target is reasonable per week.
Sales card subsections
Mirrors the dashboard: Main → Estimating → Pipeline. Estimating section appears if any of (# of Estimates Written, Estimates Written $) are toggled on. Pipeline section appears if any of (Pipeline Value, Pipeline Deals) are toggled on. (See Planned Changes — flatten Sales coming.)
Capacity targets per group
Each capacity group (defined in Settings) gets one target field here. Format depends on method:
Manual %, Time Slots, Labor Hours, Headcount: Target % field.
By Revenue ($): Weekly Revenue Goal $ field.
Stored at budget.capacityGroupGoals[groupId].
Auto-derived KPIs don't get goals (today)
Conversion Rate, Avg Repair Order, Avg Pipeline Deal, Revenue per New Client, Avg Estimate Value, Sales Close Rate (Construction), Avg Transaction Value — none have goal fields here. Their tile coloring is driven by the underlying inputs' goals. (Doc 04 Planned Change #14 changes this — auto-derived KPIs will get their own goal fields.)

Planned Changes (for rebuild)
Sales card flatten — drop Estimating subsection (carry-forward from Doc 04 #10)
Sales card on Budget & Goals removes the Estimating subsection. Pipeline subsection stays. Goal fields for # of Estimates Written, Estimates Written ($), Avg Estimate Value all sit flat in the Sales card.
KPI splits and renames affect goal fields here (carry-forward from Doc 04 #11)
"Estimates / Contracts Won" splits into separate goal fields for Contracts Won ($) and Estimates Won ($). Estimates Written tile renames drop the "Target $" wording. Revenue per New Client renamed to Contract Value per New Client.
Auto-derived KPIs get their own goal fields (carry-forward from Doc 04 #14)
New goal fields appear in their respective category cards: Conversion Rate (Marketing, %), Contract Value per New Client (Sales, $), Avg Repair Order (Operations, $), Avg Pipeline Deal (Sales, $), Avg Estimate Value (Sales, $), Avg Transaction Value (Sales, $), Sales Close Rate (Sales, %).
Implication for tile coloring: Auto-KPI tiles on the dashboard now color against their own goal, not their inputs'.
Capacity & utilization targets stay here, not Settings (carry-forward from Doc 04 #13)
Capacity group definitions (name, method, employees, working hours, capacity hours) live in Settings. Capacity targets (% goal or $ goal per group) live here on Budget & Goals. The split is intentional — setup is rare, targets reset annually.
Manual % method exception: No goal field shown for Manual % groups. The static utilization % comes from Settings and is the displayed value, not a target.
Budget & Goals is fully client-editable
Unlike Settings (coach-owned per Doc 04 #7), Budget & Goals stays open for the client to edit directly. Annual revenue target, GP %, monthly distribution (even/seasonal), YTD actuals, KPI goal fields, capacity targets — all editable by the client. Coach can also edit via Coach View.
Rationale: Setup (what gets tracked, how it's structured) is the coach's domain. Goals (what numbers we're aiming for this year) belong to the client — they own their business plan and need to be able to revise it as conditions change.
Coach role: Coach typically helps the client set initial goals during onboarding and revisits them in 1:1s. But day-to-day, the client can update without needing to ask.

Cross-doc: persistent saved state & Cancel pattern
The Save buttons on Budget & Goals (top-right "Save" and bottom-right "Save Budget & Goals") follow the portal-wide Save/Cancel pattern documented in Doc 06.
Persistent "Saved ✓" state: after a successful save, the button stays green and reads "Saved ✓" until the form is changed or the user leaves the current edit context. Replaces the current 2-second flash.
Cancel buttons paired with Save: add a Cancel button next to each Save button. Reverts the form to the most recently saved state for the current budget year — including annual revenue, GP target, YTD actuals, monthly distribution, all category goal cards, and capacity group goals.
Saved state is per-budget-year: switching to a different year (clicking a year tab or "+ {year}") resets the indicator since you're now editing a different budget. With unsaved changes on the current year, switching opens the Save / Discard Changes / Stay Here dialog before the year change completes.
Unsaved-changes guard: navigating away (top-nav clicks, Logout) with unsaved changes opens the same dialog.
"+ {next year}" button: creates a fresh budget for the next year. With unsaved changes on the current year, fires the dialog first — no silent year creation.
See Doc 06 for the full spec. This is just the cross-reference to keep all surfaces consistent.
Drop dead cogsGoal field from budget shape
The cogsGoal field still exists in the budget object shape but is silently cleared whenever cogsTargetPct is set (which is the canonical input on this page). It's still queried as a fallback in calculations but never user-facing. Remove it entirely in the rebuild.
Effect: COGS is always derived as revenueGoal × cogsTargetPct ÷ 100 for goal-display purposes. No fallback path needed.
Code cleanup: Drop the field from defBudget(), remove fallback branches in getPeriodGoalFull('cogs', ...) and getEntryGoal('cogs', ...). Confirms cogsTargetPct as the single canonical input.
YTD actuals stored month-by-month — hybrid entry (carry-forward from Doc 08)
Replace single-bucket YTD actuals (ytdRevenue, ytdCogs) with month-by-month arrays so QTD math is accurate when coaching starts mid-quarter (e.g. April or August). Doc 08 has the full decision; this card captures the Doc 05 surface changes.
Storage on the budget record: ytdRevenueByMonth: [num|null × 12] and ytdCogsByMonth: [num|null × 12]. Indices 0–11 = Jan–Dec; months past ytdThruMonth are null.
Migration: existing single-total values get auto-distributed across covered months on first load (even split when seasonType='even'; weighted by seasonPct when seasonal). Saved into the array so subsequent loads use the array directly.
Default UI — single-total entry: the existing two fields (Revenue, COGS) plus the ytdThruMonth dropdown stay as the default entry path. Coaches who don't need monthly precision use this and never see the detail.
Optional detail entry: a "Show monthly breakdown" toggle (or expand control) opens a per-month grid below the single-total fields. One row per covered month with Revenue + COGS columns. Coach can override any month inline.
Sync rule: editing the single total re-runs auto-distribution (warns before overwriting overrides). Editing a month's value updates that cell and recomputes the displayed single total as the sum.
YtdStatusBanners impact: banner math reads the array sum (same total, just sourced differently). No visible change to coach.
YtdGP auto field: derives from sum(ytdRevenueByMonth) − sum(ytdCogsByMonth).
YTD actuals + entries overlap warning (carry-forward from Doc 08)
When ytdThruMonth is set and weekly entries also exist within that same window, cumulative dashboards may double-count revenue and COGS. Surface a warning here so the coach can resolve it manually.
Where the warning appears: inline near the YTD Actuals card on Budget & Goals. Not blocking — coach can still save.
Trigger: any weekly entry with startDate on or before the last day of ytdThruMonth in the same year as the budget.
Wording (draft): "Heads up — N weekly entries exist within the YTD actuals window ([Jan–Mar]). The cumulative dashboard will add both these entries and the actuals total, which may double-count revenue. If that's not intentional, delete the entries or shorten the actuals window."
Resolution paths (coach decides): delete the offending entries via Weekly Entry, shorten ytdThruMonth, or accept the overlap.
Sun–Sat week change (carry-forward from Doc 08)
Portal-wide week boundary changes from Mon–Sun to Sun–Sat. See Doc 08 for the source-of-truth decision and full cross-doc impact list.
Doc 05 impact: goal pro-rating math that uses week boundaries (e.g. getEntryGoal for revenue uses days/daysInMo) is unaffected — it operates on entry-day counts, not week anchors. getMonthFrac, getQtrFrac, and the seasonal distribution logic are all month-/quarter-anchored, not week-anchored. No code changes here.
Per-month "weekly preview" hints (e.g. "~$10K/wk" beneath monthly goal fields): still divide by 4.33 weeks per month; that constant is unchanged.
Documentation: any reference in this doc to "Monday" as a week anchor (in examples or descriptions) updates to "Sunday" in the rebuild pass.
Note: current code behavior (above) is unchanged for now. These are notes for the eventual rebuild, not edits to apply today.
